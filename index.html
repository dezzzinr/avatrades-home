FAQ

Common questions

What is AvaTrades?

AvaTrades is a trading platform where you can trade crypto at live market prices, follow expert traders through Copy Trading, automate strategies with Trading Bots, and track personal goals set by your account manager — all from one dashboard.

How does copy trading work?

Browse a roster of trader profiles showing their risk level, win rate, returns and rating. Subscribe to a trader whose approach fits you, and your account follows their strategy for the subscription period.

Can I use trading bots without coding?

Yes. Subscribe to any of the six supported strategies — DCA, Grid, Momentum, Scalping, Rebalancing or Yield — then create your own bot instance for the asset you want, no coding required.

What's the Target feature?

Your account manager can set a personal goal amount and progress indicator that appears as a banner above your balance — a simple way to see what you're working toward.

How do deposits and withdrawals work?

Submit a deposit through one of the available funding methods and it's confirmed by our team. Withdrawal requests are reviewed the same way, with status visible from your dashboard the whole time.

Is my account secure?

Accounts require email verification, and admins have oversight tools to manage account status and keep activity safe and supported.
